      ⚗️ CCEA Unit C2 Data Leaflet & Key Formulae

      Common Relative Atomic Masses (Ar)
      H=1 | C=12 | N=14 | O=16 | Na=23 | Mg=24 | Al=27 | S=32 | Cl=35.5 | K=39 | Ca=40 | Fe=56 | Cu=64
      Reactivity Series (2.1)
      K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > [C] > Zn > Fe > [H] > Cu
      Quantitative Chemistry (2.6)
      % Element = (Total Ar of element ÷ Mr) × 100
      Atom Economy = (Mr desired ÷ Total Mr products) × 100
      Organic General Formulae (2.5)
      Alkanes: CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ | Alkenes: CₙH₂ₙ
      Alcohols: CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH
      Gas Tests (CCEA Spec)
      H₂: Lit splint → squeaky pop
      O₂: Glowing splint → relights
      CO₂: Limewater → turns milky/cloudy
      Alkenes & Bromine Water
      Alkenes (C=C double bond) turn bromine water from orange/brown to COLOURLESS.
