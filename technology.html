      📐 CCEA Unit 2 Option C [GTY23] Quick Reference

      Profile Cross-Sectional Area
      L-Angle / U-Channel / I-Beam:
      Split into rectangles (Area = L × W) OR Outer Area − Cutout
      3 Named CCEA Designers (2.42)
      • Barber & Osgerby (Olympic Torch, Tip Ton)
      • Philippe Starck (Juicy Salif, Ghost Chair)
      • Bethan Gray (craft timber, marble, brass)
      Plastic Forming Processes
      Extrusion: long profiles (angle/pipe)
      Injection: complex 3D casings (pin marks)
      Blow: hollow bottles | Vacuum: thin trays
      Thermoplastics vs Thermosets
      Thermoplastics (ABS, Acrylic, HDPE, PVC): reheat & recycle
      Thermosets (Urea/Melamine, Epoxy): heat-resistant, won't remelt
      Tolerance & QA / QC
      Tolerance = upper limit to lower limit (e.g. 50 mm ± 0.5 mm → 49.5 to 50.5 mm)
      Go/No-Go gauges check tolerance rapidly
      6 Rs of Sustainability
      Rethink • Refuse • Reduce • Reuse • Repair • Recycle
      LCA = Cradle-to-Grave environmental impact
